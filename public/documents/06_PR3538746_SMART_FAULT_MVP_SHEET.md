## 6. MVP Components

## 6.1 Fault Corridor Intelligence

Purpose:

To identify, classify, and rank structural corridors that may guide mineralization.

Inputs:

- geological maps,
- lineaments,
- DEM terrain models,
- satellite interpretation,
- field observations,
- drainage patterns,
- existing technical reports.

Outputs:

- structural corridor map,
- fault ranking,
- confidence score,
- recommended field validation zones.

---

## 6.2 Drainage Backtracking

Purpose:

To avoid treating stream-sediment samples as direct mineralization points.

Method:

- map drainage basins,
- identify upstream source areas,
- link anomalies to possible catchments,
- prioritize follow-up sampling zones.

Outputs:

- upstream catchment ranking,
- recommended stream follow-up points,
- source-probability zones.

---

## 6.3 Multi-Element Pathfinder Ranking

Purpose:

To interpret element associations in a systematic way.

Relevant element families may include:

- Pb-Zn-Sb,
- Ag-Pb-As,
- Cu-Mo-Zn,
- and other multi-element clusters depending on evidence.

Outputs:

- anomaly cluster map,
- element association matrix,
- target score by anomaly group,
- confidence level per target.

---

## 6.4 Smart Sampling Planner

Purpose:

To move from scattered sampling to structured follow-up.

Outputs:

- recommended soil grids,
- rock-chip sampling points,
- stream-sediment follow-up,
- field validation priorities,
- sampling budget estimate.

---

## 6.5 Geophysics Line Planner

Purpose:

To define where geophysics should be applied before expensive drilling.

Potential methods:

- IP,
- resistivity,
- magnetics,
- targeted geophysical profiles.

Outputs:

- proposed geophysical lines,
- method recommendation,
- priority ranking,
- expected decision value.

---

## 6.6 AI Target Score

Purpose:

To rank targets using multi-source evidence.

Indicative scoring categories:

| Criterion | Example Weight |
|---|---:|
| Structural control | 25% |
| Geochemical anomaly | 25% |
| Geological context | 15% |
| Drainage/source logic | 15% |
| Accessibility | 10% |
| Evidence confidence | 10% |

Final weights should be reviewed during implementation.

---

## 6.7 HYRION Field Evidence Capture

Purpose:

To make field evidence auditable.

Each field item should be linked to:

- GPS point,
- photo,
- sample ID,
- field note,
- collector,
- date,
- lab result,
- document version,
- access level,
- decision gate.
