## 1. Executive Summary

**Amusnaw AI SA** is proposed as a Moroccan joint **Mining-Tech SPV** designed to build the digital and governance infrastructure for intelligent mining data in Morocco.

The company is not positioned as a mining-license financing vehicle. It is positioned as a Moroccan institutional data-infrastructure company capable of organizing, validating, governing, and commercializing mining intelligence across multiple regions, mineral families, and strategic use cases.

Amusnaw AI SA launches with two ready products:

1. **Isseksi Copper Intelligence**  
   A **Ready Mining Intelligence & Governance Product** directly connected to Atlas Mining and an existing mandate. It demonstrates the company’s ability to digitize mining evidence, structure license intelligence, build controlled product rooms, and support investment-grade mining decisions.

2. **PR3538746 Smart Fault Exploration**  
   An **R&D-ready Smart Exploration MVP** for AI-assisted fault-controlled mineral targeting in the Aguelmous / Maroc Central context. It demonstrates the company’s ability to transform early-stage polymetallic exploration into structured target intelligence.

CDG is invited not as a passive financier of an exploration experiment, but as a strategic co-builder of a Moroccan mining data infrastructure layer that may evolve into a trusted gateway for critical minerals, energy-transition minerals, EV supply-chain minerals, and minerals linked to AI data-center infrastructure.

---

## 2. Strategic Thesis

Morocco does not only need more mining permits. Morocco needs a stronger digital and governance infrastructure layer capable of converting mineral evidence into investable, auditable, and decision-ready intelligence.

Many early-stage mining opportunities face the same structural problem:

- fragmented geological documents,
- inconsistent GIS data,
- weak evidence traceability,
- scattered laboratory and sampling records,
- limited investor-grade documentation,
- slow transition from field evidence to drill-ready targets,
- and insufficient digital governance for institutional partners.

Amusnaw AI SA addresses this gap by creating a structured environment where mining data becomes:

- organized,
- governed,
- searchable,
- visualized,
- ranked,
- auditable,
- and ready for institutional review.

The company’s core thesis:

> **Mining data, when governed and structured correctly, becomes strategic infrastructure.**

## 10. HYRION — Mining Evidence Infrastructure

HYRION is the evidence-governance operating layer of Amusnaw AI SA.

It is not a simple data room.

It is designed to become the operating system for governed mining evidence.

## 10.1 HYRION Functions

| Function | Institutional Value |
|---|---|
| Evidence Registry | Every license, map, sample, assay, report, and decision is registered |
| Data Permissions | Access is controlled by role, phase, and clearance |
| Field Chain of Custody | GPS, images, samples, lab results, and field notes are linked |
| Budget-to-Work Tracking | Funds are connected to verified technical work |
| Decision Gates | Technical escalation is prevented without evidence |
| Investor Reporting | Monthly and quarterly reports can be generated |
| Acquisition Readiness | Mining cases become easier to review or acquire |
| Settlement Logic | Rights, obligations, milestones, and exit events are documented |

## 10.2 Why HYRION Is Strategic

Even if one field case remains early-stage, HYRION creates scalable value by turning mining evaluation into a repeatable, auditable, governed process.

For CDG, this is the infrastructure layer that can scale beyond Isseksi and PR3538746.
