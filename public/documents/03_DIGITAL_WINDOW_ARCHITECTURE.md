## 1. Core Objective

The digital window is the institutional interface through which **Amusnaw AI SA** will be presented to CDG and strategic stakeholders.

It is not a public marketing website.

It is a controlled institutional environment designed to:

- present Amusnaw AI SA as a Moroccan Mining-Tech Data Infrastructure SPV,
- show the two launch products,
- host the CDG Strategic Entry Memo,
- organize the document package,
- support staged access,
- prepare for NDA-based deeper review,
- and later evolve into the HYRION-powered mining evidence infrastructure.

---

## 2. Strategic Role of the Digital Window

The digital window must solve a strategic problem:

CDG should not receive scattered PDFs, WhatsApp explanations, or informal project descriptions.

CDG should enter a controlled interface that immediately communicates:

1. **This is an institutional SPV proposal.**
2. **The product is not a mining license.**
3. **The objective is mining data infrastructure.**
4. **There are two ready launch products.**
5. **The seed request is disciplined and infrastructure-based.**
6. **The company is designed to be governable, scalable, and acquirable.**

The window must turn the conversation from:

> “Please review our documents.”

into:

> “Here is the institutional environment through which Amusnaw AI SA can be understood, reviewed, structured, and co-built.”

## 18. Access Level Architecture

### Level 0 — Pre-NDA / Public-Lite

Accessible without sensitive details.

Includes:

- Amusnaw AI basic definition.
- CDG-aligned thesis.
- Two product overview.
- Contact path.

### Level 1 — Controlled Institutional Access

Requires approved access.

Includes:

- CDG Strategic Entry Memo.
- Executive brief.
- Product one-pagers.
- Seed commitment summary.

### Level 2 — NDA Access

Requires NDA.

Includes:

- Isseksi deeper product details.
- PR3538746 technical details.
- HYRION architecture.
- QASSAS methodology.
- Cap table details.

### Level 3 — CDG Review Access

Reserved for CDG / approved institutional reviewers.

Includes:

- Full financial structure.
- IP contribution files.
- Step-up acquisition pathway.
- Legal drafts.
- Product demo environment.
- Governance dashboard.
