This file defines the complete document package required to present **Amusnaw AI SA** to CDG through a controlled digital institutional window.

The goal is to create a disciplined document architecture that helps CDG understand:

1. What Amusnaw AI SA is.
2. Why CDG is the right strategic co-builder.
3. Why Isseksi is a ready product.
4. Why PR3538746 is a smart exploration MVP.
5. Why HYRION is the governance infrastructure.
6. Why QASSAS is the exploration intelligence layer.
7. How USD 350,000 is used as a Seed Infrastructure Commitment.
8. How the SPV can become acquirable by CDG over time.
9. How the digital window will organize institutional communication.

## 2. Document Package Principle

All documents must follow the same operating discipline:

# Strategic + Planning + Execution

## Strategic

Each document must explain:

- why it matters,
- why CDG should care,
- why Morocco benefits,
- why Amusnaw AI SA is the right vehicle,
- why the subject is not a speculative mining claim.

## Planning

Each document must define:

- structure,
- stakeholders,
- ownership,
- access level,
- roadmap,
- budget,
- dependencies,
- governance.

## Execution

Each document must identify:

- what will be built,
- what will be delivered,
- who contributes,
- what CDG sees,
- what decision is required,
- and what happens next.
