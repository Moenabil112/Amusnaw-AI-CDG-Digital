## 4. 90-Second Pitch

**Amusnaw AI SA** is proposed as a Moroccan joint Mining-Tech SPV co-built with CDG to establish the digital and governance infrastructure for intelligent mining data in Morocco.

The company launches with two products:

1. **Isseksi Copper Intelligence** — a ready mining intelligence and governance product connected to Atlas Mining and an existing mandate.
2. **PR3538746 Smart Fault Exploration** — an R&D-ready MVP for AI-assisted fault-controlled mineral targeting.

The proposed CDG role is a **Seed Infrastructure Commitment** of USD 350,000 for 30% participation, not exploration CAPEX.

The six-month objective is to deliver:

- HYRION Mining Data Infrastructure v1,
- Isseksi Product Room,
- PR3538746 Target Book,
- QASSAS Morocco v1,
- and a CDG scale decision pack.

The long-term objective is to build a Moroccan gateway for strategic mineral intelligence covering critical minerals, energy-transition minerals, EV supply-chain minerals, and AI data-center infrastructure minerals.

## 8. Likely CDG Questions and Suggested Answers

## Q1. Is this a request to finance a mining license?

**Answer:**  
No. The proposal is not to finance a mining license. It is to co-build Amusnaw AI SA as a Moroccan Mining-Tech Data Infrastructure SPV. The two mining cases are launch products used to demonstrate the infrastructure, not the sole basis of the company.

---

## Q2. Why should CDG be involved?

**Answer:**  
Because the project requires institutional co-building, not only money. CDG can provide governance credibility, strategic structuring, and national-scale discipline. The proposed entry gives CDG a serious early position in a company designed to organize intelligent mining data for Morocco.

---

## Q3. What does CDG receive for USD 350,000?

**Answer:**  
CDG receives a proposed 30% participation in Amusnaw AI SA, governance visibility, access to two launch products, HYRION reporting, QASSAS Morocco outputs, right of first review on scale-up, and a staged acquisition option subject to milestones and legal documentation.

---

## Q4. Why is the amount USD 350,000?

**Answer:**  
Because the first phase is not drilling or mine development. It is a six-month Seed Infrastructure Commitment to establish the SPV, package Isseksi, prepare PR3538746 as an MVP, deploy HYRION v1, adapt QASSAS to Morocco, and prepare a CDG scale decision pack.

---

## Q5. Is Isseksi owned by Amusnaw AI SA?

**Answer:**  
Isseksi is presented as a product connected to Atlas Mining and an existing mandate. It is not presented as an Amusnaw-owned license. Its role is to demonstrate Amusnaw AI SA’s capacity to productize and govern mining intelligence through a controlled digital environment.

---

## Q6. What is Atlas Mining’s role?

**Answer:**  
Atlas Mining is the mining operating and field partner. It contributes mining credibility, Isseksi access, operational knowledge, and 22 years of mining experience across more than four countries.

---

## Q7. What is ZYNTRA Deeptech?

**Answer:**  
ZYNTRA Deeptech is positioned as a Saudi technology company contributing the AI product layer, deeptech architecture, software logic, and scalable interface/product thinking.

---

## Q8. What is AKANIL’s contribution?

**Answer:**  
AKANIL contributes intellectual property, field intelligence, trust architecture, knowledge transfer, African strategic value-chain understanding, and the context behind HYRION and QASSAS.

---

## Q9. What is the founder’s role?

**Answer:**  
Mohamed Nabil acts as Mining-Tech Originator & Field Intelligence Architect. His role is to assemble the companies, technologies, mining relationships, field intelligence, and CDG pathway into one governable Moroccan SPV.

---

## Q10. What is HYRION exactly?

**Answer:**  
HYRION is the evidence-governance operating system. It organizes mining evidence, controls access, tracks decisions, links budgets to work, prepares reporting, and makes mining cases institutionally reviewable.

---

## Q11. What is QASSAS?

**Answer:**  
QASSAS is the exploration intelligence layer. It converts geology, structure, stream-sediment data, and field evidence into target ranking, sampling plans, geophysics logic, and drill-readiness recommendations under expert review.

---

## Q12. Is PR3538746 a proven resource?

**Answer:**  
No. PR3538746 is not presented as a proven resource. It is an R&D-ready smart exploration MVP for AI-assisted fault-controlled target generation.

---

## Q13. What happens after six months?

**Answer:**  
CDG receives the scale decision pack. The options are: continue development, expand to more Moroccan cases, introduce an industrial partner, or activate a step-up pathway depending on milestones.

---

## Q14. What is the acquisition path?

**Answer:**  
CDG may be granted a staged step-up acquisition right: 30% at seed, option to increase to 51% after 18 months, and up to 85% after 36 months, subject to milestones, valuation review, governance approval, IP terms, and legal documentation. The founder retains a 15% strategic continuity stake.

---

## Q15. Why should this be a Moroccan SA?

**Answer:**  
Because Amusnaw AI SA is designed as a serious institutional vehicle for mining data infrastructure, contributed IP, strategic shareholders, governance, and future acquisition. The SA structure is more suitable for institutional confidence and step-up ownership.
