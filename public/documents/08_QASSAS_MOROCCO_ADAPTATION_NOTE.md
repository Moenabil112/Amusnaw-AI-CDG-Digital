QASSAS must be adapted to Morocco, not copied mechanically from another context.

The Moroccan adaptation should account for:

- Atlas and Anti-Atlas geological diversity,
- Maroc Central polymetallic systems,
- copper and base metal districts,

- drainage-controlled geochemistry,

The adaptation should be modular, evidence-based, and expandable.

## 5. QASSAS Morocco Modules

## 5.1 Fault Corridor Intelligence

Purpose:

To identify and rank structural corridors that may control mineralization.

Inputs:

- geological maps,
- DEM,
- satellite imagery,
- lineaments,
- mapped faults,
- valley orientation,
- field observations,
- previous reports.

Outputs:

- fault corridor map,
- structural ranking,
- target confidence score,
- field validation priority.

Application:

Especially relevant for PR3538746, where the project is positioned as smart fault exploration.

---

## 5.2 Drainage Backtracking

Purpose:

To trace geochemical anomalies from stream-sediment samples back toward likely source zones.

Why it matters:

Stream-sediment anomalies do not equal ore bodies. They must be understood through drainage systems.

Inputs:

- stream network,
- topography,
- sample points,
- anomaly values,
- catchment basins.

Outputs:

- upstream source zones,
- anomaly catchment ranking,
- recommended follow-up points,
- priority drainage segments.

---

## 5.3 Multi-Element Pathfinder Ranking

Purpose:

To interpret geochemical associations rather than isolated element values.

Possible associations:

- Pb-Zn-Sb,
- Ag-Pb-As,
- Cu-Mo-Zn,
- polymetallic hydrothermal signatures,
- local pathfinder patterns depending on evidence.

Outputs:

- element association matrix,
- cluster ranking,
- anomaly intensity score,
- confidence class,
- follow-up recommendation.

---

## 5.4 Smart Sampling Planner

Purpose:

To move from random sampling to evidence-based sampling design.

Outputs:

- soil sample grid,
- rock-chip points,
- stream follow-up points,
- sampling density,
- field priority list,
- estimated campaign effort.

Value:

Reduces waste and improves decision quality.

---

## 5.5 Geophysical Line Planner

Purpose:

To identify where geophysics should be used before drilling.

Possible methods:

- IP,
- resistivity,
- magnetics,
- targeted profiles based on structural and geochemical logic.

Outputs:

- recommended geophysical lines,
- method rationale,
- priority,
- expected decision value,
- follow-up interpretation plan.

---

## 5.6 AI Target Score

Purpose:

To combine multiple evidence layers into a target ranking.

Indicative evidence layers:

- structure,
- geochemistry,
- lithology,
- alteration,
- drainage-source logic,
- geophysics potential,
- access,
- evidence confidence.

Output:

A ranked list of targets:

| Target | Structure | Geochemistry | Access | Confidence | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| T-01 | High | Medium | Good | Medium | 1 |
| T-02 | Medium | High | Medium | Medium | 2 |

---

## 5.7 Drill Collar Recommender

Purpose:

To support scout drilling decisions only after validation.

Inputs:

- target ranking,
- geophysics,
- field validation,
- structural orientation,
- terrain/access.

Outputs:

- proposed collar locations,
- orientation,
- depth class,
- target rationale,
- confidence level.

Important:

This module should not be presented as immediate drilling.

It is part of staged drill-readiness logic.

---

Purpose:
