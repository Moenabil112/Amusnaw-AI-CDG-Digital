## 9.2 License & Evidence Layer

Purpose:

To organize license-related and supporting evidence.

Content:

- License identity.
- License documents.
- Location evidence.

- Access level.

- Allow preview/download only under access level.

## 9.3 GIS Layer

Purpose:

To demonstrate spatial intelligence.

Content:

- License boundary.
- Site location.
- Supporting map layers.
- Evidence-linked features.
- Future expansion to multiple license layers.

Digital behavior:

- Interactive map placeholder in v0.1.
- GeoJSON/GIS integration in future HYRION version.
- Controlled display of sensitive spatial data.

## 9.4 Technical Evidence Layer

Purpose:

To summarize the technical basis without overwhelming CDG.

Content:

- Processing or mining relevance.
- Technical report index.
- Existing validation status.
- Required next technical review.

Digital behavior:

- Document cards.
- Short executive technical summaries.
- Deep files under NDA.
